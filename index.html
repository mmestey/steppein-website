FAQ

Questions, answered

Is SteppeIn free for workers? +
Yes. Core marketplace access is free for workers. Create a profile, find nearby work, and accept opportunities without paying platform fees.
Is this a job board? +
No. SteppeIn is a marketplace for getting agricultural work done — post the need, match workers, accept the engagement, complete the work, and handle payment and reputation.
How does payment work? +
When supported, payment is facilitated after work is completed and confirmed. Exact payout options and timing are shown in the app as features launch.
When does the app launch? +
SteppeIn is in early access. Join the waitlist and we’ll notify you when mobile apps open in your area.
